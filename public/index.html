FLAPPY DUDE

Your face is the bird.

Camera & recording

This game uses your front-facing camera / webcam so your face replaces the bird sprite. Your face is cropped to a consistent square while you play.

  • The live camera feed is shown as the flapping character.
  • Each play session is recorded as a gameplay replay, including your face and microphone audio (reactions, laughs, groans).
  • When the run ends, that replay plays back immediately so you can review it.
  • Nothing is uploaded until you choose to — pressing Upload video to community posts your name, score, and replay (video + audio) to the public Community Log so other players can watch.

By continuing you allow webcam and microphone access and session recording (video + audio). Your replay stays on your device until you choose to upload it to the public community log.
